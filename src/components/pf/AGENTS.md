Author pages show the Pink product but keep the portfolio upload box (owner decision defaulted). A page mounts one live book only: the profession demonstrations load their PDF into the hero's viewer through LandingExampleProvider. Why: plan 3, no click-to-view gates and one WebGL context per page.
- Public pages use MarketingHeader (Examples, Products, For authors, Pricing; owner decision 1 of plan 2, default) and follow docs/design-standard.md; signed-in pages use AppHeader. Why: visitors from search had no route to pricing, examples or products.
- The home page lost its full plan comparison, "Why send a link instead of a PDF?" (now on /pdf-to-link) and the price comparison (now on competitor pages); owner decision 2 of plan 2, default. Why: pricing sat before the product was shown and the page was about 10,000 px tall.
- Marketing pictures appear only once listed in lib/portfolia/marketing-assets.ts (MarketingImage/MarketingClip render nothing otherwise); plans/owner-shot-list.md says what to make. Proof (lib/portfolia/proof.ts) ships empty: real figures and quotes with permission only (owner decision 3). Demonstration portfolios stay labelled as demonstrations on /examples (owner decision 4).
- /examples and /guides are static routes, so both are in RESERVED. An account that already owns one of those names would lose its root address; check the database before uploading.
- The homepage example sizes its book from the column's measured height (--pf-home-stage-h set in HomeWorkspace), not the window. Why: on short desktop windows the text column is taller than the window and a window-sized book left an empty band below it.
- Page animations are stickers with `sticker: "animation"` and a stored moving picture (page-extras.ts, page-animation.ts, PageAnimation.tsx). Frames are read once and drawn from the page's clock, flat over still pages and on the paper of turning ones (sticker-layer.tsx), so a looping animation keeps going through a turn. The library is Google's Noto animated emoji (CC BY 4.0, public/licenses/noto-animated-emoji.txt): the catalogue ships with the site and a picked picture is copied into the portfolio's own files, so readers never load anything from Google. Why: owner wants children's books with looping animations that sit seamlessly on the page, with a free searchable library.
- In the 3D book, animations are always drawn on the paper (the sticker layer stays on for them while pages are still; BookView), never in the flat overlay. Why: owner found the flat copy was lit differently from the page in Simple and Studio and jolted when a turn handed over.
