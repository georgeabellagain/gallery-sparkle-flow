- The product editor (ProductEditor.tsx) mirrors the portfolio editor's layout (top bar, full-height pf-editor-stage book, bottom Upload/Customise/Photo shoot/Share bar, pf-panel side card with a Customise menu) but keeps its own state and save flow. Why: creators should move between portfolios and products without relearning the editor.
- The first button in the editor's bottom bar is always the product's next step (Upload PDF, Choose preview pages, Publish, Get links; see nextProductStep in lib/products/plan.ts), and uploading makes a first preview (cover and first spread) automatically. Why: owner asked for creating a product and getting its links to be as simple as possible.
- Plans: Free is a try-out (one draft product); publishing and customer links need Personal or Business (lib/products/plan.ts, enforced in functions.ts and the product_studio_plans migration). A product already online stays online if the plan ends, so buyers keep access. Why: owner's decision that the studio is a paid feature with a free preview.
- Customer links are shown once and stored only as a hash; never return token_hash or secret to the browser. Why: owner requires the links to be secure.
- Plan 1 columns (cover_file, slug, extra_files, delivery, stamp) arrive with migrations the owner applies by hand: saveProduct drops a missing column and reports it (`unsupported`), list/serve code retries without it, and the editor shows "Available after the next update". Never make an existing screen depend on one.
- Owner decisions (defaults): readable addresses, statistics, buyer-name stamps and extra files come with publishing (Personal and Business); automatic delivery is Business only with 500 emails a day per product; the delivery email is from "<title> via Portfolia" <noreply@portfolia.site>; Etsy delivery is not built (no simple order webhook); stamp text is "Licensed to <name>"; extra types are zip, epub, png, jpg, mp3 and txt (no video, because of size).
- Delivery keys and customer-link tokens are shown once and stored as SHA-256 hashes; the Shopify signing secret is stored but never returned; ownerProduct strips `delivery` from the row. A repeated order (same product, source and order ref) never sends again.
- Homepage 'For digital products' tab shows the published Pink product's preview file in Studio only (ProductDemo.tsx, falls back to pink28); product preview always includes page 1 and is picked by spreads. PreviewChrome (Preview label, Buy the full product, end-of-preview card) is shared by the homepage and /product/$id, and both readers show page arrows. Why: owner's request for a consistent, clickable shop preview.
- A product's price, Buy button wording and seller name live in `appearance` (allowlisted plain text in model.ts, read with productListing), so they need no migration. Readers always keep the flipbook; Scroll and Page by page are the seller's choice (`modes`). Why: owner asked for the customisation gaps between portfolios and products to be closed.
- Products have page animations too (appearance.animations, ProductAnimations.tsx): files live in product-files and are served by /api/public/product-animation/<id>; shoppers get only those on preview pages, renumbered (productStickers). Storage paths never reach the public page. Placing is by page number and sliders, not dragging. Why: owner asked for animations on products, for children's books sold as digital products.
