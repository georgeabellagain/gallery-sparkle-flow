## Plan 1: Digital Product Studio features
All six features are built and tested in code. None could be tried against a real database, a signed-in account, a real shop or a real email (see "Not checked" below).

- **A, cover thumbnails:**
  - Each preview now makes a WebP of public page 1 (480 px, or 360 px if needed, at most 300 KB). Older products get one quietly the next time the editor opens.
  - The covers show on the products page. Public covers are served only for published products, through `/api/public/product-cover/<id>`, proxied rather than redirected.
  - The product page's static `head` is unchanged. A share card with the cover would need a route loader; I left it out.
- **B, readable address, QR code and embed:**
  - New "Your address" field in Links (Suggest button; reserved words and ids refused). A taken address says "That address is taken. Try another."
  - `/product/<slug>` and `/product/<id>` both work. Customer links keep the id.
  - QR code and Embed buttons are on the preview card only. `/embed/product/<slug-or-id>` always shows the preview, even with a customer cookie.
  - Framing: `src/server.ts` already allows every `/embed/` path, so it was not changed.
- **C, statistics:**
  - Daily counts of preview views (once per browser session), Buy clicks, customer opens and downloads (once per download, not per range request).
  - Bots are ignored, and no IPs, cookies or ids are stored.
  - New "Statistics" panel (four tiles, buy rate from 20 views, 30-day bars). Products-page cards show "N views · N buy clicks in 30 days", from one call for all products.
- **D, buyer name on downloads:**
  - Optional field when making a customer link. Each page of the downloaded PDF then carries "Licensed to <name>", 7 pt grey at the foot, and the PDF Subject holds the same line.
  - The server stamps files up to 20 MB. Larger files are stamped in the buyer's browser.
  - A stamped link never gets an unstamped file through `download=1`. A file that can't be stamped returns 422 with "This file can't be stamped. Contact the seller."
- **E, extra files:**
  - Up to 5 ZIP, EPUB, PNG, JPG, MP3 or TXT files (Personal and Business), in "File & publishing".
  - Customers get an "Included with your purchase" card. Shoppers see only "Also includes N extra files".
  - Always served as attachments (octet-stream, `nosniff`, sandbox, safe file name). A customer session is needed. HTML, SVG, JS and EXE are refused in the code and in the database.
  - Removed extras and replaced covers are deleted after a save, best effort. The extras migration adds an owner delete policy for non-PDF files only.
- **F, automatic delivery (Business):**
  - New "Automatic delivery" panel: Gumroad, Shopify or Other (Zapier, Make, Payhip…). Etsy is shown but disabled.
  - Each shop has its own steps and an address to paste. A delivery key is shown once and stored as a hash; the Shopify signing secret is never sent back.
  - There is a "Send a test / add an order by hand" form and an orders list with Resend and Switch off. "Add an order by hand" is also linked from Links.
  - Endpoint `/api/public/product-delivery/<id>/<source>`:
    - Reads at most 256 KB of raw body.
    - Answers 401 for a bad key or signature, 404 for anything unavailable (without saying why), 429 over 60 orders a minute, and 200 for accepted and repeated orders.
    - Never echoes the request.
  - A repeated order sends nothing. A refund switches its link off. The daily cap is 500 (orders past it are listed "Not sent: daily limit").
  - Emails come from "<title> via Portfolia" <noreply@portfolia.site> through the same sender as lead emails.
  - Data export now includes products, their links (without tokens) and orders. Account deletion already removes them, because products cascade from the account.
- **Plan copy:**
  - The pricing comparison, the Personal and Business feature lists and the three product landing pages list the new features.
  - The Etsy FAQ answer on the product pages is rewritten, as plan 1 F4 asks.
  - `docs/digital-product-studio.md` and `src/components/products/AGENTS.md` are updated.
