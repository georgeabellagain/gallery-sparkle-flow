- Portfolia is a focused PDF-hosting prototype (brief v2): no editor, templates or discovery — scope was deliberately reduced by the user.
- PDF upload allowances are plan-based: Free publishes up to 30 MB and Personal up to 100 MB (store.ts constants, mirrored in supabase/migrations/20261019150000_larger_upload_limits.sql). New uploads can be previewed up to PREVIEW_LIMIT_MB (150) and stay browser-only until they fit; publishing offers a smaller web copy (web-copy.ts resizes large RGB JPEGs) or an upgrade. Replacing a published PDF keeps the strict limit.
- Portfolios live in the `portfolios` table (full Portfolio JSON in `data`) and files in private `portfolio-files/<uid>/<key>`; store.ts keeps a localStorage/IndexedDB cache and cloud.ts syncs it. Why: links must work on any device.
- Public portfolio pages load via getPublicPortfolio (publishable read + admin-signed file URLs); drafts are never returned.
- PDF viewer renders with pdfjs-dist directly; don't import pdf_viewer.css (Lightning CSS rejects its relative urls) — text-layer rules are copied into styles.css.
- Personalised portfolios use root paths (`/name`); `/p/code` remains the permanent free address and `/u/name` remains compatible with old links.
- Payments: Paddle via connector gateway; subscriptions table synced by webhook at /api/public/payments/webhook; useAccount mirrors subscription into the portfolio plan field, which syncs to the account.
- Portfolio QR codes are generated locally in the browser from the active public link and downloaded as high-resolution PNGs; no third-party QR service receives portfolio URLs.
- Flipbook rendering lives in BookView.tsx and book-scene.ts. One persistent Three.js scene lights both stationary pages and the turning sheet. Simple/Studio settings are viewer-local; Studio uses physically shaded paper and bundled CC0 photographic backdrops. Phones use the same two-page book and a camera that follows each page; corner cues are desktop-only and hide before turns. Page textures load before animation and are bounded to eight cached PDF pages.
- Visitors stream published PDFs by HTTP Range (remote-pdf.ts: PDFDataRangeTransport, 64 KB chunks, no auto-fetch); the creator's own IndexedDB copy is still used whole. Supabase signed URLs answer ranges but hide Accept-Ranges/Content-Range cross-origin, so the length comes from Content-Range or HEAD, never pdf.js's own URL loader; expired links are re-signed through getPublicPortfolio (setPublicUrlRefresher). Streamed documents (isStreamedPdf) open scroll on the pages in view, draw scroll pages and thumbnails as they near, sample image density on pages 1-3 and preload at most seven book pages. The password/expiry file route forwards Range to a 60-second signed link. Why: a 30-100 MB PDF took 23-72 s on a phone before the first spread; streamed it is about 5-8 s.
- Page pictures (page-images.ts): after publishing or replacing a PDF, the owner's browser draws every page in a worker (page-images.worker.ts, pdf.js + OffscreenCanvas; main-thread fallback) as WebP, or JPEG where WebP can't be encoded (Safari), at 1600 and 2560 px on the long side (each half of a landscape spread gets that detail; max 4096), into portfolio-files/<uid>/<pdfKey>_pages/<n>-<size>.<format>, then saves a manifest on PdfFile.pageImages (pdfKey, page sizes, picture sizes, the PDF's own web links). The dashboard and editor run usePageImageBackfill for any published, synced portfolio without a current manifest (one at a time). Visitors whose links for every picture are signed open on page-image-doc.ts, a stand-in pdf.js document: pages draw the smallest picture that covers the screen, links come from the manifest, selectable text comes from <n>-text.json beside the pictures (manifest `text: true`; TextLayer only sizes its box for pdf.js's own PageViewport, so pageImageText sizes it), and the streamed PDF opens only for zoom beyond 1.2x the largest picture, a picture that fails to load, or text of older manifests. The flipbook composes picture pages straight from a decoded ImageBitmap, caps each face at the picture's detail and keeps PICTURE_FACES (10) decoded instead of the PDF budget (up to ~21 on desktop, 23 on phones); PdfViewer starts downloading pages 1-3 and the last before the 3D scene builds. Pictures count as derived copies of the PDF, not toward any allowance (user decision, Oct 2026); they follow the PDF in retention, replacement, deletion (deleteBlobs), export (pagePictureFiles) and account deletion (which empties folders). Keys with a folder are allowed only by safeAssetKey. Why: phones waited 5-21 s for pdf.js before the first spread; with pictures it is about 0.3-1.6 s and 0.06-0.3 MB.
- Studio start-up: every lighting file has a 256x128 lighting-N-preview.hdr (scripts/make-lighting-previews.ts; tests/lighting-preview.test.ts checks each exists and keeps the full file's average colour). The book scene and page Studio light with the preview first and swap in the full photograph when it arrives (book: never while a page turns); lighting is fetched and converted in environment.worker.ts (environment-pixels.ts, no three.js) and PdfViewer starts it as soon as Studio is the opening look. Page shaders compile with renderer.compileAsync; paint() is held while they compile (never during a turn or capture) and BookView reveals the book after shadersReady(). Why: Studio waited for up to 2 MB of lighting parsed on the main thread and a blocking shader compile; on a phone link it now opens in about 1 s instead of 2.3 s.
- Published embeds use `/embed/<code>` and receive route-specific framing and no-index response headers in the server entry. Why: only the compact viewer should be frameable and excluded from search.
- Profile details and portfolio appearance are edited on separate routes; own portfolio cards open the appearance editor while explicit Preview opens the visitor view. Why: creator navigation should distinguish identity from presentation.
- Flipbook camera zoom and cursor anchoring live in the persistent book scene; the viewer toolbar owns the shared zoom value. Why: wheel and button zoom must remain coordinated while turns can smoothly reset the camera.
- Desktop page-turn drag targets cover the full left and right book sides at normal zoom; zoomed dragging remains camera panning. Why: readers can pull pages anywhere without losing positioning controls.
- Viewer mode and Flipbook appearance availability are creator-owned portfolio settings; visitors may switch only among enabled choices, while Studio material and lighting remain fixed by the creator. Why: shared links must preserve the intended presentation.
- A lost flipbook graphics context shows a readable canvas page and restores the scene when graphics return; the homepage example uses smaller page textures. Why: temporary GPU failures must not hide the portfolio or leave a permanent warning.
- Website links use one shared PDF-page collection; full-page readers map split-leaf coordinates and display icons inside the committed artwork layer. Why: every viewing style must preserve placement without duplicating links or showing destination links before a page is ready.
- Page tabs may store an optional edge-centre fraction; geometry clamps the whole tab to the edge and omission retains automatic spacing. Why: creators can reposition tabs without changing existing portfolios.
- Plan copy (feature lists, comparison table, FAQ, yearly saving) lives only in src/lib/portfolia/plans.ts and is rendered by PricingPlans/UpgradeModal/dashboard. Why: the homepage, pricing page and upgrade dialog previously disagreed and undersold Personal; every listed Personal item must stay enforced in code.
- Personalised addresses are claimed in the Share panel (AddressSettings) via the name_available RPC; RESERVED must include every static route name. Why: a username matching a static route can never be reached.
- Hardback model: every page block top sits at PAGE_TOP; an open cover lies flat just below the paper (OPEN_COVER_Z) and a closed one on its board above the block (coverArtZ). A turning cover is drawn by binding.turnCover as a real board carrying both printed faces; its hinge height moves between the two resting heights with a brief lift. Why: the old raised inside cover let the next paper turn land underneath it (red flash) and made boards/spine jump at every cover turn.
- Page-block thickness per side follows leaf position (sideShare/stackDepth via canvas dataset leafCount), and front/back boards take colours from their own covers. Why: the book's thickness shifts as it is read and no side ever pops.
- Studio adds gutter shading (gutter-shade.ts) measured from the spine axis in book space; Simple stays exact to the artwork. The photo shoot adds contact shadows/corner occlusion (photo-grounding.ts) and reader-matched page relief for spreads.
- Viewer toolbar buttons are flat inside the shared bar with hairline group dividers; selected states use ink (foreground) and sliders use the leaf accent, never stock blue.
- Book details (book-details.ts): hardback boards and spine use a shared procedural book-cloth normal/roughness map (per-material clones keep their own repeat); hardbacks have headbands lying across the head and foot of the gutter; an optional ribbon (viewer.ribbon/ribbonColour, hardback and soft cover) drapes onto the table, short enough to stay in the reader's frame. Why: tactile cues of a real bound book without touching page turns. Textures are DataTextures so tests run without a DOM.
- Photo shoot camera presets (SHOOT_PRESETS) include a Flat lay: the book lies on the floor far from the wall (FLAT_Z) with lights moved alongside, and its own elevation range (SHOOT_ELEVATION). The photo ribbon is built in world space so it drapes the same way in either layout.
- The editor waits for hydration before redirecting a missing portfolio to the dashboard. Why: reloading /edit signed-out bounced creators away from a portfolio saved on the device.
- Photo shoot opens from the editor's bottom bar (Upload PDF · Customise · Photo shoot · Share), not from Customise. Everyone can stage and preview; Download is Personal (photoExportAction, UpgradeModal reason "Photo shoot export"). Why: the preview sells the feature; the gate is client-side only, like HD video, so it is not tamper-proof.
- Photo-shoot grounding decals are DataTexture superellipses that fade to zero at their rim (shadowAlpha); only the thin contact decal is dark, and the wall decal darkens toward the floor. Why: canvas-blurred rectangles showed square edges in Leaning and Flat lay.
- The ribbon can mark a page (viewer.ribbonPage, "Marks" in Book style & page turns). ribbonPlacement compares the marked spread's gutter (leaves left of it) with the resting leaves: on the open marked spread it lies down the gutter valley from the headband and off the foot; otherwise it leaves the correct block (right before the mark, left after) at a depth matching its position. Resting pages straddle a turning sheet, so a turn only ever sees a tail inside the block and nothing can clip the sheet. No page keeps the old tail. Ribbon geometry has thickness, rounded edges, a V-notched end, twist only where it hangs, satin sheen (Studio only) and a vertex-alpha contact shadow. The photo shoot lays it across the spread when the shown spread contains the marked page.
- Notes, tabs and links are added and edited on the main editor's page (ScrapbookQuickPanel + LiveScrapbookPage, shared selection through LiveScrapbookContext); the scrapbook dialog stays for pictures, fonts and inside surfaces. Notes may rotate ±30° (Foldout.rotation, snapping to straight within 2°); the slant is baked into note geometry, not the group, so page turns carry it. Why: creators place things where they see them; the data model and Save flow are unchanged.
- Page tabs and contents links (PageJump, kind "jumps": a box over a contents line with a target page) turn once, straight to their spread: BookView.move takes a destination leaf, and every page passed over rides the turning sheet with its tab (planTabs marks leaves from the sheet's front to its back). Reduced motion places the page directly. In Scroll and Page-by-page a contents link scrolls or steps to its page.
- Website links in the reader show an "opens a website" badge and "Opens host" hint on hover/focus (always shown on touch screens). Why: the painted logo did not look clickable.
- The portfolio file endpoint serves only PDF and raster image types as themselves; anything else downloads as application/octet-stream. Password unlock rate limits key on cf-connecting-ip. Why: stored content types are uploader-controlled, and the first x-forwarded-for entry can be forged.
- Share panel: "Share to" sends the portfolio link to LinkedIn, Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Pinterest or email through each network's public share page (social-share.ts), so the post shows the link preview card. Networks never accept a video through a link: "Share with a flip-through video" (Personal, reuses VideoExportPanel) uses the system share sheet where files can be shared (navigator.canShare), otherwise downloads the video, copies the caption with the link and opens the network's composer.
- Webhook events keep their Paddle occurrence time in subscriptions.updated_at and an older event never overwrites a newer one. Why: a late "active" must not revive a cancellation; no migration needed.
- Access settings use ownerHasPersonal like public delivery, so past-due customers are treated the same everywhere.
- Accounts can download their data (JSON) and delete themselves from the dashboard (deleteAccount: files, portfolios with cascades, feedback, auth user). A still-renewing plan must be cancelled first; billing records are kept for tax.
- Public portfolio profile panels carry a "Report this portfolio" mailto link; Terms describe content rules and the reporting/takedown route. /scrapbook-preview is a dev prototype and returns 404 in production.
- Incoming notes revealed by a turn stay hidden until the sheet has started to lift (progress > 0.03). Why: during the pan before a cover opens they showed through the closed hardback cover.
- Ribbon routing: it always leaves from (or lies on) a side with paper under it, never a hardback cover lying open; from the foot it lies over the board's overhang and round its edge (straight, barely twisted) before dropping, so it never enters a board. Per-point contact/facing values map to control points by curve parameter, not arc length. Headbands show only on an open book; a closed one hides them in its case. Why: the tail lay on the inside back cover and cut through the board overhang, and headband ends poked past the spine of a closed book.
- Ribbon foot route (footRun): past the paper edge (which overhangs the block by 2 mm), hanging in front of the page block clear of the headband, over the board's overhang and round its edge, then onto the table; with no board (photo flat lay, whose pages rest on the floor) it settles straight onto the floor. A turn that leaves or reaches the marked spread drives the ribbon each frame (binding.setRibbonTurn/turnRibbon): leaving forward or arriving backward it rides on the turning sheet; otherwise it stays on the resting right page, sinking under a landing sheet or rising as one lifts. Marking a cover page marks the open spread beside it (ribbonGutterFor, ribbonSpreadPage). Why: the ribbon vanished during turns, met the paper edge and headband at the foot, and marking page 1 put it on a closed cover.
- The open ribbon rests on the highest paper under its width (underRibbon) plus RIBBON_LIFT, because pages are drawn as a coarse 33x17 grid; from raised paper it drapes off the foot in one slope that clears the board corner (a vertical hang is invisible from the reader's top-down camera). Carried by a turning page, every ribbon vertex keeps its height above the paper along the sheet's normal (carryRibbon), so the strip bends with the page across its width. Headbands are the ribbon colour with soft, low-contrast ribbing (no hard stripes) and a slimmer bead. Why: the ribbon z-fought and cut into curved paper, and the striped headbands read as a texture glitch.
- Beside a hardback cover lying open (first and last spreads) the headbands hide and an open ribbon moves off the spine (RIBBON_COVER_INSET), so no silk shows on the inside of a cover. During a turn the hanging end slides from its starting drape to its finishing one (ribbonHang/blendHang: resampled to matching points, kept relative to the moving foot near the page and blended absolutely on the table). Why: ribbon colour appeared on the inside covers, and the hanging end jumped at the end of a turn.
- Google Ads conversions (src/lib/conversions.ts on top of Lovable's ads-consent.ts): a sign-up is reported when an email sign-up creates a new identity or a brand-new account (created in the last 15 minutes) first signs in, including Google; a Personal subscription is reported on the dashboard after checkout once the webhook row exists, valued 3 or 25 GBP with the Paddle subscription id as transaction_id. Each is offered once per browser and only sent where consent allows. The cookie banner renders from the root (never inside /embed/), and a Cookie settings link in the footer and Privacy page reopens it.
- Task landing pages (src/lib/portfolia/task-pages.ts, one thin route each) target what people search to do with a PDF: pdf-to-link, share-pdf-online, pdf-to-website, host-pdf, pdf-qr-code, ucas-portfolio, how-to-send-a-portfolio, send-large-pdf, lookbook-maker, canva-flipbook. They reuse SeoLanding with the inline upload box, appear in SEO_LINKS and RESERVED, and are checked by tests/task-pages.test.ts (titles <= 72 chars, descriptions 110-160). Why: Google autocomplete/Trends (Oct 2026) showed 'pdf to link', 'share pdf' and 'pdf to website' far outrank 'pdf flipbook'; copy must only claim shipped features.
- Spotlights (PageSpotlight in page-extras.ts, kind "spotlights"): a creator marks a box on a page with an optional caption; readers see a magnifier there and open a lightbox (PageSpotlights.tsx) that draws that region of the PDF at screen resolution with pdf.js (page pictures first, then the streamed PDF via fullPdf), with arrow keys between spotlights, Escape/backdrop to close and focus kept inside. Free keeps 3 (publicPortfolioPlan trims the public copy), Personal 24. Why: detail-heavy portfolios (architecture, illustration) need a sharp closer look without uploading extra images.
- "Someone opened your portfolio" emails (Personal, opt-in Portfolio.notifyOnOpen, stripped from public data): after recordVisit stores a visit the browser calls portfolioOpened; open-notify.server.ts ignores bots and the owner, requires that visit's portfolio_events row from the last 5 minutes, and claims a send through claim_open_notification (supabase/migrations/20261022120000_open_notifications.sql), so each portfolio gets at most one email every 6 hours and 5 a day, with later opens counted into the next. The email (email-templates/portfolio-opened.tsx) carries time (UTC), Cloudflare country and device type only; no IP is stored. Why: visits are inserted by browsers, so the server must not trust the caller.
- GIFs on pages become looping silent videos in the browser (gif-to-video.ts: WebCodecs ImageDecoder + mediabunny, H.264 MP4 or VP9/VP8 WebM, white behind transparency, <= 60 s, <= 1280 px) and then use the page-video system; PageVideo.silent hides the speaker button. Browsers without WebCodecs are asked for an MP4.
- Reader controls show for 4.5 s when a hover-capable reader arrives (ARRIVAL_CONTROLS_MS) before resting out of sight. On phones the viewer toolbar is one scrolling row without zoom buttons (pinch zooms), and it reserves only the width of the icons on the left.
- The homepage example is the owner's published fashion lookbook (code adu2v, homepage-examples.ts), opened on pages 4-5; the owner adds its features in the editor like any portfolio. Field Notes (published as 67wkw, with a copy shipped in public/examples/field-notes, field-notes-example.ts) is kept for other uses; its book and extras are generated by scripts kept outside the repo (example-flipbook/source).
- Tabs on a turning sheet hang from its real edge, read from the live sheet mesh every frame (book-scene passes an edge sampler to tabs.turn), so they follow every turn style, including Soft fold and its pointer turns.
- A dragged Soft fold turn follows the pointer like competitors' (turn-curve pointerFold): the point grabbed on the page edge stays under the pointer, the paper folds along the line halfway between them (shifted by half the roll so the grabbed point lands exactly), constrainFoldPointer keeps it bound at the spine, and release carries the grabbed point to the far side or home. It is written live (writePointerFold); clicks, keys and tabs keep the baked Soft fold.
- Simple shades the page under a turning sheet (turn-shade.ts, resting pages only): lightly as the sheet lifts, fading by mid-turn, and darkening the page it closes on, spreading from the spine. Studio relies on its real shadows.
- Homepage starts a working Simple 3D preview and moving backdrop before scheduling Studio warm-up in idle time. Keep the loader over Simple until its camera introduction has settled, shaders are ready and a frame is painted. Start a 20-second visible-time first showing at that reveal, then switch only when Studio is ready and alternate every 10 seconds (home-look-cycle.ts). Bottom Simple/Studio tabs pause cycling on selection and remain usable in fullscreen. Hidden readers pause their tour, video and background animations; scenes stay mounted. Offscreen/hidden tabs pause the cycle; interaction/fullscreen freezes the current look. Both tours share a clock. There is no comparison divider.
- The loader is flat semicircular page-fan line art with one turning line and the Portfolia wordmark underneath (book-loader.tsx); reduced motion keeps it still.
- Reusable looks live in owner-only `portfolio_saved_looks` (JSON array per portfolio), not public `portfolios.data`. cloud.ts splits/merges them and reloads after MFA verification; saved-looks.ts allowlists visual fields so copying never changes PDF content, page interpretation, reader modes, identity or access. Uploaded backgrounds participate in retention/migration through portfolioAppearanceKeys. Public responses also strip the library defensively.
- Customisation has exact lighting percentages, descriptive HDRI names, a preview-only test turn, input-layout diagrams, background inheritance actions and selected-item positioning. preview-turn.ts queues one short-lived editor action; leaving Book & turns cancels it. Scrapbook uses actual page ratios for alignment and optional page-edge/centre snapping; Alt bypasses drag snapping and fine-nudges, Shift coarse-nudges.

- Studio motion uses a bounded drawing surface during turns, orbit, pan and wheel zoom, restoring reading detail after the camera settles; capture stays full quality. Book-binding layout caches unchanged spreads so camera frames do not rebuild/upload page-layer matrices. Free shared readers show a subtle Make your own link beside the Portfolia credit; Personal and unbranded embeds do not.

- Corner turn cues are 8–24 px and soft-light blended into the underlying page so they inherit its artwork and lighting rather than showing a cream flap.
